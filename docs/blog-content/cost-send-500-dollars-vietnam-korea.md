## 🔍 Understanding the Real Cost

### What Makes Up the Total Cost?

When you send $500 to Vietnam, you pay for:

```
Total Cost = Transfer Fee + Exchange Rate Markup + (Hidden Fees)
```

**1. Transfer Fee** (visible)
- What the app shows you upfront
- Usually ₩3,000-₩5,000 for apps
- ₩20,000-₩30,000 for banks

**2. Exchange Rate Markup** (hidden)
- The difference between the "real" rate and the rate you get
- This is where services make most of their money
- Can be 0.5% to 3% of your transfer

**3. Hidden Fees** (sometimes)
- Receiving bank fees in Vietnam
- Intermediary bank fees (for wire transfers)
- Usually only with bank transfers
