# Send Money to MoMo Vietnam from Korea

Over 200,000 Vietnamese workers in Korea send money home every month. MoMo, Vietnam's leading mobile wallet with 50+ million users, has become the most popular way for families to receive and use that money instantly.

This guide shows you how to get money from Korea into your family's MoMo wallet quickly and cheaply.

---

## The Short Answer: How It Works

You cannot send money directly to MoMo from Korea. Instead:

1. You send money to a **Vietnamese bank account** using a Korean remittance service
2. Money arrives in the bank (typically 4-24 hours)
3. If the bank is **linked to MoMo**, money transfers automatically
4. Your family uses the money immediately through MoMo

**The key step**: Tell your family to link their bank account to MoMo. It takes 2 minutes in the MoMo app and makes everything automatic.

## Step-by-Step: Send Money to MoMo

### What You Need

**Your side (Korea)**:
- Alien Registration Card (ARC) or passport
- Korean phone number
- Korean bank account

**Recipient side (Vietnam)**:
- Vietnamese bank account number
- Bank name (Vietcombank, BIDV, Techcombank, etc.)
- Full name (exactly as on Vietnamese ID)
- Vietnamese ID number (CCCD)
- Phone number

### The Process

**Step 1: Compare and choose a service**

Check [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) for current rates. Pick the service offering the highest VND for your amount.

**Step 2: Register (first time only)**

Download the app, enter your personal info, upload ARC photos, take a selfie. Verification takes 1-24 hours.

**Step 3: Add recipient**

Enter your family member's bank details. Double-check the name spelling—it must match their Vietnamese ID exactly.

**Step 4: Enter amount and review**

Check the exchange rate, fee, and total VND your family will receive. Rates are usually locked for 10-30 minutes.

**Step 5: Pay and track**

Pay via bank transfer (cheapest) or card. You'll get a reference number to track the transfer.

**Step 6: Money arrives**

Your family receives money in their bank account. If linked to MoMo, it transfers automatically.

## How Your Family Links Bank to MoMo

This is a one-time setup that takes 2 minutes:

1. Open MoMo app
2. Go to "My Wallet" → "Linked Bank Account"
3. Select their bank
4. Enter account number
5. Verify with OTP from bank

After linking, every transfer you send will appear in MoMo automatically.

**Supported banks**: Vietcombank, BIDV, Techcombank, VietinBank, ACB, MB Bank, and most major banks.
