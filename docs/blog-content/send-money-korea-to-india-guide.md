## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the cheapest way to send money to India from Korea?

The cheapest way depends on current rates. Generally, **Gmoneytrans** offers the best INR exchange rates, while **E9Pay** has the lowest fees for small amounts. Always compare on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) before sending. For a ₩1,000,000 transfer, you can save ₩20,000-30,000 by choosing the right service.

### How long does it take to send money to India from Korea?

Most digital services deliver to India within 1-2 business days. Same-day delivery is possible with services like Hanpass and Coinshot if you send during business hours. Bank transfers typically take 2-3 business days.

### What documents do I need to send money to India from Korea?

You need: 1) Alien Registration Card (ARC) or passport, 2) Korean phone number, 3) Korean bank account. Your recipient needs: bank account number, IFSC code, and the name must match bank records exactly.

### Is it safe to send money to India through Korean apps?

Yes, licensed Korean remittance services like Hanpass, GME Remit, E9Pay, and others are regulated by Korean financial authorities. They use encryption and follow strict security protocols. Always use official apps from app stores.

### Can I send money to UPI in India from Korea?

Some services support UPI transfers, but bank account transfers are more common. Check with your chosen provider for UPI availability. Bank transfers are reliable and widely supported.

### What is the maximum amount I can send to India from Korea?

Limits vary by service and verification level. Typical limits are:
- Per transfer: ₩3,000,000 - ₩10,000,000
- Annual: ₩50,000,000 - ₩100,000,000
- Higher limits available with additional verification

### Do I pay tax on money sent to India from Korea?

Digital transfers typically don't have the 1% Korean remittance tax. Indian recipients don't pay tax on remittances under certain limits. Korean foreign workers may qualify for tax reductions. [Learn about tax incentives](/blog/remittance-tax-incentive-guide-foreign-workers-2026).

### Which Indian banks can receive money from Korea?

All major Indian banks are supported: SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, PNB, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, and more. You'll need the correct IFSC code for the specific branch.

### Can E-9 visa workers send money to India?

Yes, E-9 visa workers can send money to India using services like E9Pay, Hanpass, GME Remit, and others. E9Pay is specifically designed for E-9 workers with easier registration and customer support.
