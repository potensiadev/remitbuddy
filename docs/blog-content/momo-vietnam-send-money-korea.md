## 📋 Can You Send Money Directly to MoMo from Korea?

This is the most common question from Vietnamese workers in Korea. The answer is: **not directly, but effectively yes** through bank linking.

### How It Works

Most Korean remittance services send money to **Vietnamese bank accounts**, not directly to mobile wallets. However, MoMo users can link their bank account to MoMo, and money arrives in MoMo almost instantly.

**The Process:**
1. ✅ You send money to your family's Vietnamese bank account using a Korean remittance service
2. ✅ Money arrives in the bank account (typically within 1-24 hours)
3. ✅ Money automatically transfers to linked MoMo wallet (instant if linked, or your family can transfer manually)
4. ✅ Your family uses money immediately through MoMo

> 📌 **Important**: Tell your family to link their bank account to MoMo. This takes 2 minutes in the MoMo app and makes receiving money from Korea seamless.

### How to Link Bank Account to MoMo

Your family in Vietnam should:

**Step 1:** Open MoMo app
**Step 2:** Go to "My Wallet" or "Profile"
**Step 3:** Select "Linked Bank Account"
**Step 4:** Choose their bank (Vietcombank, BIDV, Techcombank, etc.)
**Step 5:** Enter bank account number
**Step 6:** Verify with OTP from bank

After linking, money sent to the bank account can be transferred to MoMo instantly for free.

**Before sending money:**
1. ✅ Call your family to confirm bank account number

### Avoid Common Scams

**Scam type 1: Fake customer service**
- 🚨 Scammer claims to be from remittance service
- 🚨 Asks for password or PIN
- 🚨 Says your account has problem
- ✅ **Real support NEVER asks for passwords**
- ✅ Call official number yourself, don't trust caller

**Scam type 2: Phishing messages**
- 🚨 SMS or email with link to "verify account"
- 🚨 Website looks real but URL is slightly different
- 🚨 Asks you to enter login details
- ✅ **Always go directly to official app**
- ✅ Never click links in messages

**Scam type 3: Too-good-to-be-true rates**
- 🚨 Unknown service offers rate much better than market
- 🚨 No proper license or regulation
