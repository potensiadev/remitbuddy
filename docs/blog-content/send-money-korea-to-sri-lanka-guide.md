## 💰 Understanding Fees and Exchange Rates for Sri Lanka

The total cost of sending money to Sri Lanka has two parts: **transfer fees** and **exchange rate markup**. Understanding both helps you save money.

### Transfer Fees

This is the flat fee charged by the remittance service.

**Typical fees for Korea to Sri Lanka transfers**:

| 🏢 Service Type | 💰 Fee Range |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Digital apps | 3,000 - 10,000 KRW per transfer |
| Bank transfers | 20,000 - 40,000 KRW per transfer |
| First transfer | Free or discounted |
| Cash pickup | 10,000-15,000 KRW (higher fees) |

**Tips to reduce fees**:
- ✅ Look for promotional offers
- ✅ Compare on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) before sending

> 💡 **Learn more**: Read our guide on [how to pay the lowest remittance fee](/blog/how-to-pay-lowest-remittance-fee) with money-saving strategies.

---

### Exchange Rate Markup (KRW to LKR)

This is the hidden cost. The service buys LKR at one rate and sells to you at a slightly different rate.

**How it works**:
- Market mid-rate: 1 KRW = 0.24 LKR (example)
- Service's rate to you: 1 KRW = 0.235 LKR
- Markup: About 2%

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- 2% markup = 10,000 KRW lost to exchange rate
- This can be MORE than the transfer fee

**How to find the best rate**:
- ✅ Compare services on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)
- ✅ Check the actual LKR amount recipient receives
- ✅ Don't just look at the fee - calculate total cost
- ✅ Exchange rates change daily

According to [Exchange Rates data](https://www.exchangerates.org.uk/Sri-Lankan-Rupee-LKR-currency-table.html), the USD/LKR exchange rate was 328.37 on May 29, 2026. For KRW to LKR, rates vary by service provider.

---

### Total Cost Comparison Example

**Sending 500,000 KRW to Sri Lanka**:

| 🏢 Service Type | 💰 Transfer Fee | 📊 Exchange Rate (LKR) | LKR Received | Total Loss |
|-----------------|-----------------|------------------------|--------------|------------|
| Service A | 3,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.230 LKR | 114,310 LKR | 5.8% |
| Service B | 7,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.240 LKR | 118,320 LKR | 3.2% |
| Bank | 30,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.228 LKR | 107,160 LKR | 11.4% |

**Service B is cheapest** even with higher fee, because the exchange rate is much better.

> 📌 **Key lesson**: Always compare the **total LKR amount received**, not just the transfer fee.

**❌ Phishing messages**:
- Don't click links in SMS or emails

- Check sender address carefully

**❌ Too-good-to-be-true rates**:
- If rate is much better than all other services, be suspicious
- Stick to licensed, regulated services
- Use services listed on RemitBuddy

**❌ Fake apps**:
- Only download from official app stores
- Check app developer name
- Read reviews before downloading
- Look for verified badge
