### How Bank Transfers Actually Work

When you send money through a Korean bank to Vietnam, here's what happens:

```
Day 1: Korean bank processes your request
       ↓
Day 1-2: Money goes to intermediary bank (usually in US or Singapore)
       ↓
Day 2-3: Intermediary sends to Vietnam correspondent bank
       ↓
Day 3: Vietnamese bank credits recipient's account
```

**That's 3-4 banks touching your money.** Each one adds time and fees.

### How Remittance Apps Work (Much Faster)

```
Minute 1: You send via Hanpass/E9Pay
       ↓
Minute 5-10: Direct transfer to Vietnam partner
       ↓
Minute 10-30: Family receives money
```

**Only 2 parties involved.** That's why it's faster.

## ⚡ Solution 3: Avoid Bad Timing

### The Best and Worst Times to Send

| Timing | Speed | Recommendation |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Tuesday-Thursday, morning** | ⚡ Fastest | Best time to send |
| **Monday morning** | 🔶 Good | Weekend backlog clearing |
| **Friday afternoon** | 🔴 Slow | May not process until Monday |
| **Saturday-Sunday** | 🔴 Slowest | Banks closed in Korea |
| **Korean holidays** | 🔴 Very slow | +2-3 days delay |
| **Vietnamese holidays (Tet)** | 🔴 Very slow | Banks closed in Vietnam |

### Why Tuesday-Thursday Is Best

- Monday: Banks clearing weekend backlog
- Tuesday-Thursday: Normal processing, no rush
- Friday: Risk of rolling into weekend
- Weekend: Most banks don't process

> ⚠️ **Tet Warning**: During Vietnamese New Year (late January/early February), expect delays of 3-5 days even with apps. Send money BEFORE Tet.

## ⚡ Solution 4: Fix Recipient Information Errors

Wrong details = automatic delay. Sometimes your money gets stuck for days.

### Most Common Mistakes

| Mistake | What Happens | How to Fix |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| Name doesn't match bank account | Transfer rejected or held | Use EXACT name on bank account |
| Wrong account number | Sent to wrong person or returned | Triple-check every digit |

### Before You Send: Verification Checklist

- [ ] Recipient name matches bank account EXACTLY
- [ ] Account number confirmed by recipient
- [ ] Bank name is correct
- [ ] Phone number is current (for MoMo)

## ❓ FAQ: Vietnam Transfer Speed

### Why does my bank transfer take 3 days?

Bank wire transfers go through the SWIFT network, passing through 2-3 intermediary banks. Each bank adds processing time. Remittance apps use direct partnerships, skipping intermediaries.

### Can I speed up a transfer that's already sent?

Unfortunately, no. Once sent through a bank, you must wait. For future transfers, use an app instead.

### Is MoMo faster than bank transfer?

Yes. MoMo receives instantly because it's a digital wallet. Bank accounts require the Vietnamese bank to process the deposit.

### Why did my Hanpass transfer take 2 hours instead of 10 minutes?

Possible reasons:
- Vietnamese bank was processing slowly
- You sent during peak hours
- Large amount triggered review
- First-time recipient verification

### Do transfers work on weekends?

App transfers (Hanpass, E9Pay): Yes, usually work 24/7
Bank transfers: No, processed next business day

### What's the fastest way to send money to Vietnam?

Hanpass or E9Pay to MoMo wallet: 5-10 minutes average.

### Will transfers be faster during Tet?

No, slower. Vietnamese banks have limited operations during Tet. Send money before the holiday.
