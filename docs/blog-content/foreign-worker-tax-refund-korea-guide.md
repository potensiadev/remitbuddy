## 💰 What is a Tax Refund for Foreign Workers?

A **tax refund** happens when you paid more income tax than you actually owe. The Korean tax office (National Tax Service) returns the extra money to you.

### How Tax Works in Korea

Every month, your employer takes income tax from your salary. This is called **withholding tax**. The amount taken is based on estimates.

At the end of the year, you file a **year-end tax settlement** (called 연말정산 in Korean). This calculates your real tax based on:
- Your actual annual income
- Deductions you can claim
- Credits you qualify for

If you paid more than you owe, you get a refund. If you paid less, you owe more tax.

### Who Can Get a Tax Refund?

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Visa Type** | E-9, H-2, E-7, E-1 to E-10, F-4, F-5 visa holders |
| **Employment** | Working with valid employment contract |
| **Tax Paid** | Income tax was withheld from your salary |
| **Residence** | Spent at least 183 days in Korea during tax year |
| **Deductions** | Have eligible expenses like insurance, medical costs, education |

Most E-9 factory workers and H-2 workers can get refunds if they:
- Paid for medical expenses
- Paid for health insurance
- Have pension contributions
- Made donations
- Have dependent family in Korea

## 📝 Step-by-Step: How to File for Tax Refund

The process is called **year-end tax settlement** or 연말정산 (yeonmal jeongsan). Here's how to do it:

### Step 1: Gather Your Documents

Collect these documents before you start:

**Required for Everyone:**
- ✅ Alien Registration Card (ARC) or passport
- ✅ Employment certificate from your employer
- ✅ Income withholding receipts (원천징수영수증)
- ✅ Bank account information (for refund deposit)

**For Deductions:**
- ✅ National Pension payment proof
- ✅ Health Insurance payment proof
- ✅ Medical expense receipts (hospital, pharmacy, dental)
- ✅ Credit card spending summary
- ✅ Insurance premium receipts
- ✅ Donation receipts (if any)

> 💡 **Tip**: Keep all receipts in a folder throughout the year. This makes filing much easier.

### Step 2: Get Help from Your Employer

Most employers help with year-end tax settlement for employees.

**What your employer can do:**
1. Provide your income and tax withholding information
2. Give you the official withholding receipt (원천징수영수증)
3. Submit your tax settlement to the tax office
4. Help translate documents if needed

**Timeline:**
- Tax settlement period: **January to March** each year
- Deadline for filing: **March 10**
- Refunds typically paid: **March to April**

> ⚠️ **Warning**: If your employer doesn't help, you must file yourself. Don't miss the deadline or you lose your refund.

### Step 3: File Online or Visit Tax Office

You have two options:

#### Option A: Online Filing (Easiest)

1. Visit **Hometax** website: [www.hometax.go.kr](https://www.hometax.go.kr)
2. Click **English** at the top right corner
3. Register account using your ARC number
4. Click **"Year-End Tax Settlement"** menu
5. Enter your information and deductions
6. Upload scanned documents
7. Submit electronically

**Pros:**
- ✅ Can do from home
- ✅ English available
- ✅ Faster processing
- ✅ Can track status online

**Cons:**
- ❌ Need computer and internet
- ❌ Must scan documents
- ❌ Some workers find it confusing

#### Option B: Visit Tax Office in Person

1. Find your local tax office using the [tax office locator](https://www.nts.go.kr/english/main.do)
2. Bring all original documents
3. Go during weekday business hours (9 AM - 6 PM)
4. Take a number and wait
5. Staff will help you file

**Pros:**
- ✅ Staff can help directly
- ✅ Translation services available at some offices
- ✅ Can ask questions

**Cons:**
- ❌ Must take time off work
- ❌ May wait 1-2 hours
- ❌ Language barrier possible

> 💡 **Tip**: Many community centers for foreign workers offer free tax filing help. Ask your labor support center (외국인근로자지원센터).

### Step 4: Submit Your Deductions

This is the most important step. More deductions = bigger refund.

**Enter each deduction carefully:**

| Deduction | Where to Find Information |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Pension** | Payment statement from National Pension Service |
| **Health Insurance** | Payment notice from National Health Insurance |
| **Medical** | Hospital/pharmacy receipts showing your name |
| **Credit Card** | Annual usage statement from card company |
| **Insurance** | Premium payment receipts |
| **Donations** | Receipt from registered charity |

> 📌 **Important**: Only claim real expenses. False claims can result in penalties and problems with visa renewal.

### Step 5: Submit and Wait for Refund

After filing:

1. **Review confirmation**: Check you received submission confirmation
2. **Track status**: Use Hometax to check processing status
3. **Wait for deposit**: Refund deposited to your Korean bank account
4. **Timeline**: Usually 2-4 weeks after filing

| Event | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| File tax settlement | January - March 10 |
| Review by tax office | 1-3 weeks |
| Refund calculation | 1 week |
| Money deposited | Mid-March to April |

> ✨ **Pro Tip**: File early (January) to get your refund faster. Files submitted in March may take longer to process.

**💬 Q: When can I file for tax refund?**

A: Year-end tax settlement period is **January to March 10** each year. You file for the previous year's income. For example, in January-March 2027, you file for 2026 income. File early (January) to get your refund faster.

A: After filing, the tax office reviews your submission (1-3 weeks) and deposits the refund to your bank account. Most workers receive refunds in mid-March to April. Early filers (January) get refunds faster than those who file in March.
