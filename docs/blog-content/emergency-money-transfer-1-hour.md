## ⚠️ What to Do When Transfer Is Delayed

Even fast services can face delays. Here's how to handle it.

### Expected vs Problem Delays

**Normal delays** (don't panic):
- ✅ Sent during evening: Processed next day
- ✅ Sent during weekend: Processed Monday
- ✅ Holiday in recipient country: +1-2 days
- ✅ First-time transfer: +4-6 hours for checks

**Problem delays** (take action):
- ❌ No update after 2 hours during business hours
- ❌ Transfer stuck in "processing" over 6 hours
- ❌ Status hasn't changed in 24 hours
- ❌ Recipient not found or rejected

### Step-by-Step: What to Do

**Step 1: Check transfer status (5 minutes)**
1. Open remittance app
2. Find transfer history
3. Read status message carefully
4. Check for error messages
5. Screenshot current status

**Step 2: Verify details (5 minutes)**
1. Check recipient name spelling
2. Verify account/wallet number
3. Confirm bank/wallet service correct
4. Check mobile number accurate

**Step 3: Contact customer service (10-30 minutes)**

**Have ready before calling**:
- Reference/tracking number
- Transaction date and time
- Amount sent
- Recipient details
- Screenshot of status

**What to ask**:
- Current transfer status
- Expected completion time
- Reason for delay
- Action needed from you
- Escalation options

**Step 4: Contact recipient (5 minutes)**

Ask recipient to:
- Check bank/wallet app
- Check SMS messages
- Verify account status
- Check if account is active
- Contact their bank if needed

**Step 5: Escalate if no resolution (1-3 days)**

If transfer stuck for 48+ hours:

1. **Email customer service**: Send detailed complaint with all screenshots

3. **File complaint**: Contact Korean Financial Supervisory Service (금융감독원)

**💬 Q: How much extra do emergency transfers cost?**

**💬 Q: Which service is fastest for Philippines emergency transfers?**
