- Demand for VND increases
- Rates get worse
- You pay more for the same VND

**Solution**: Send your Tet money in **early January**, before the rush.

## ❓ FAQ: Vietnam Transfer Timing

### What day has the best exchange rate for Vietnam?

Generally, **Tuesday through Thursday** have the most stable and competitive rates. Wednesday is often best due to peak market liquidity.

### What time should I send money to Vietnam?

**2:00-4:00 PM Korean time** is optimal. Both Korean and Vietnamese markets are active, giving you the best rates.

### Should I wait for a better rate?

If your rate is within 1% of the monthly average, it's fine to send. Waiting too long for a "perfect" rate often backfires - rates can go either direction.

### Do rates change on weekends?

Remittance apps usually lock weekend rates on Friday evening. These rates are often slightly worse. If possible, send on weekdays.

### When should I send money for Tet?

Send in **early to mid-January**, well before Tet. Rates worsen 2 weeks before the holiday as demand spikes.

### Is there a best month to send money to Vietnam?

**March-June and September** tend to be stable. Avoid late January (Tet), December (year-end), and major holiday periods.

### How much can I save with good timing?

For ₩1,000,000 monthly, good timing can save **₩150,000-300,000 per year** through better rates and lower fees.
