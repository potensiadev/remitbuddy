## 📱 Method 1: MoMo Wallet (Best Option)

### What Is MoMo?

MoMo is Vietnam's largest mobile wallet with 40+ million users. It's like having a bank account on your phone.

**Your family needs**:
- A Vietnamese phone number
- A smartphone (any Android or iPhone)
- MoMo app downloaded
- Basic ID verification completed

### Why MoMo Is the Best Option

| Feature | MoMo | Bank Account |
|---------|------|--------------|
| Opening | Free, 5 minutes | Need documents, visit branch |
| Receiving money | Instant | 10-30 minutes |
| Withdrawing cash | 30,000+ ATMs | Bank ATMs only |
| Paying bills | Yes, in-app | Need to visit or transfer |
| Shopping | Millions of stores | Limited |

### How to Send Money to MoMo

**Step 1: Help family set up MoMo (one-time)**

Have your family member:
1. Download MoMo app from app store
2. Register with their phone number
3. Complete basic verification (ID card photo)
4. Link a payment method (optional for receiving)

**Step 2: Send money from Korea**

Using **Hanpass**:
1. Open Hanpass app
2. Select "Vietnam"
3. Choose "MoMo Wallet" as delivery method
4. Enter recipient's MoMo phone number
5. Enter amount
6. Review and confirm
7. **Done** - money arrives in 5-10 minutes

Using **E9Pay**:
1. Open E9Pay app
2. Select Vietnam transfer
3. Choose MoMo option
4. Enter phone number and amount
5. Complete payment
6. **Done** - arrives in 5-10 minutes

### What Your Family Can Do with MoMo

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Withdraw cash** | Any MoMo point, ATM, or partner store (30,000+ locations) |
| **Pay bills** | Electric, water, internet, phone - all in-app |
| **Shop online** | Thousands of online stores accept MoMo |
| **Pay in stores** | Scan QR code at millions of locations |
| **Transfer to others** | Send to other MoMo users or bank accounts |

### MoMo Withdrawal Locations

Your family can get cash at:
- Circle K (convenience stores)
- Vinmart / Vinmart+
- Bach Hoa Xanh
- FPT shops
- Any store with "MoMo" sign
- ATMs (some banks)

> 💡 **Tip**: There's probably a MoMo withdrawal point within 1km of your family, even in rural areas.

### MoMo Fees and Limits

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Receiving money | Free |
| Withdrawal at store | Usually free |
| ATM withdrawal | 1,000-3,000 VND |
| Daily withdrawal limit | Up to 20,000,000 VND |
| Monthly withdrawal limit | Up to 100,000,000 VND |

## 💵 Method 2: Cash Pickup

### What Is Cash Pickup?

You send money online. Your family walks into a partner location and picks up cash with an ID and pickup code.

**Your family needs**:
- Valid ID (CMND/CCCD)
- Pickup code (you give them)
- Knowledge of pickup location

### Cash Pickup Partners in Vietnam

| Service | Pickup Network | Locations |
|---------|----------------|-----------|
| GME Remit | Various | Major cities |
| MoneyGram | Banks, shops | Nationwide |
| Western Union | Banks, agents | Nationwide |

> ⚠️ **Note**: Cash pickup options from Korea are more limited than from US/Europe. Check availability.

### How Cash Pickup Works

**Step 1: You send money (Korea)**
1. Choose a service with cash pickup option
2. Select "Cash Pickup" as delivery method
3. Enter recipient details (name as on ID)
4. Complete payment
5. Receive pickup code/reference number

**Step 2: Family picks up (Vietnam)**
1. You send them the pickup code
2. They go to pickup location
3. Show ID matching the name
4. Give pickup code
5. Receive cash

### Cash Pickup Pros and Cons

**Pros**:
- ✅ No smartphone needed
- ✅ No bank account needed
- ✅ Cash in hand
- ✅ Works in cities and some towns

**Cons**:
- ❌ Higher fees (₩8,000-15,000)
- ❌ Must travel to pickup point
- ❌ Business hours only
- ❌ Some ID required
- ❌ Limited availability from Korea

### When Cash Pickup Makes Sense

- Older family members without smartphones
- Very rural areas with pickup agents but no MoMo
- Family prefers physical cash handling
- One-time emergency transfer

## 📞 Method 3: Mobile Top-up

### What Is Mobile Top-up?

You send phone credit directly to a Vietnamese phone number. Not money transfer, but useful for emergencies.

**Your family needs**:
- Active Vietnamese phone number
- That's it

### How Mobile Top-up Works

1. You select mobile top-up on a service
2. Enter family's Vietnamese phone number
3. Choose amount of credit
4. Pay in KRW
5. **Instant** - credit appears on their phone

### When Mobile Top-up Is Useful

| Situation | Why It Helps |
|-----------|--------------|
| Family ran out of phone credit | They can call/text you |
| Emergency communication | Fast and instant |
| Data for using MoMo | Need internet to use wallets |
| Small recurring support | Keeps them connected |

### Mobile Top-up Limitations

- ❌ Not actual money (phone credit only)
- ❌ Can't withdraw as cash
- ❌ Limited to phone services
- ❌ Not good for general expenses

### Available Services

Some remittance apps offer Vietnam mobile top-up. Check if available in your app.

## 📱 Helping Family Set Up MoMo (Step-by-Step)

### What They Need

- [ ] Any smartphone (Android 5.0+ or iPhone 6+)
- [ ] Vietnamese SIM card with active number
- [ ] Vietnamese ID card (CMND or CCCD)
- [ ] 10 minutes

### Setup Process (Guide Them via Video Call)

**Step 1: Download MoMo**
- Android: Search "MoMo" on Google Play
- iPhone: Search "MoMo" on App Store
- Install (free)

**Step 2: Register**
1. Open MoMo app
2. Enter phone number
3. Receive SMS verification code
4. Enter code in app
5. Create PIN (6 digits) - **remember this!**

**Step 3: Basic Verification**
1. Tap profile icon
2. Select "Verify account"
3. Take photo of ID card (front and back)
4. Take selfie
5. Wait 1-5 minutes for approval

**Step 4: Test Receiving**
1. Send small amount (₩50,000) first
2. Confirm they received it
3. Help them learn to withdraw

### Tips for Helping Non-Tech Family

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't know how to download" | Video call and guide step by step |
| "I forgot my PIN" | Reset via phone verification |
| "The app is confusing" | Focus only on receiving and withdrawing |
| "I'm scared of technology" | Show them it's just like getting money at a store |

## ⚠️ Common Issues and Solutions

### Issue 1: Family Can't Verify MoMo Account

**Cause**: ID card issue or photo quality
**Solutions**:
- Retake ID photo in good lighting
- Make sure full ID is visible
- Try different background (plain white)
- Contact MoMo support if persistent

### Issue 2: Money Sent But Not Received

**Check**:
1. Did you enter correct phone number?
2. Is their MoMo account verified?
3. Check your app for transfer status

**Solution**: Contact Hanpass/E9Pay support with transaction ID

### Issue 3: Family Can't Find Withdrawal Point

**Solutions**:
- Use MoMo app map to find nearest point
- Search "điểm giao dịch MoMo" + their area
- Try convenience stores (Circle K, Vinmart)
- Withdraw smaller amount at ATM

### Issue 4: Withdrawal Limit Reached

**MoMo daily limit**: 20,000,000 VND
**Solution**: Withdraw over multiple days, or increase limit by completing advanced verification

## ❓ FAQ: No Bank Account Transfers

### Can I send money to Vietnam without a bank account?

Yes. Use MoMo wallet (best option), cash pickup, or mobile top-up. MoMo is recommended because it's free to set up and your family can withdraw cash at thousands of locations.

### What's the cheapest way to send money without a bank?

**MoMo wallet** via Hanpass or E9Pay. Fees are only ₩3,000-5,000, same as bank transfers but no bank account needed.

### Does my family need a smartphone for MoMo?

Yes, MoMo requires a smartphone (any basic Android or iPhone works). If they don't have one, consider cash pickup or helping them get an affordable smartphone.

### How does my family withdraw cash from MoMo?

At any of 30,000+ MoMo points: Circle K, Vinmart, Bach Hoa Xanh, FPT shops, and many local stores. Just show their MoMo app and withdraw.

### Is MoMo safe?

Yes. MoMo is Vietnam's largest fintech company, regulated by the State Bank of Vietnam. Over 40 million Vietnamese use it.

### Can elderly family members use MoMo?

Yes, with help. The app is in Vietnamese and relatively simple. Help them set it up via video call, and teach them only the basics: receiving and withdrawing.

### What if there's no MoMo point in their village?

MoMo has extensive coverage, but in very remote areas:
- Check the MoMo map for nearby points
- Consider cash pickup if available
- They may need to travel to nearest town
