### Exchange Rate Markup (KRW to BDT)

This is the hidden cost. The service buys BDT at one rate and sells to you at a slightly different rate.

**How it works**:
- Market mid-rate: 1 KRW = 0.080 BDT (example)
- Service's rate to you: 1 KRW = 0.078 BDT
- Markup: About 2-3%

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- 2.5% markup = 12,500 KRW lost to exchange rate
- This can be MORE than the transfer fee

**How to find the best rate**:
- ✅ Compare services on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)
- ✅ Check the actual BDT amount recipient receives
- ✅ Don't just look at the fee - calculate total cost
- ✅ Exchange rates change daily
- ✅ Check [Bangladesh Bank exchange rates](https://www.bb.org.bd/en/index.php/econdata/exchangerate) for reference

According to the [World Bank Remittance Prices Database](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/), comparing services can save you 3-5% per transfer.

### 2.5% Government Incentive Explained

Bangladesh government adds 2.5% to every official remittance to encourage legal money transfers.

**How it works**:
- You send: 500,000 KRW = 40,000 BDT (at example rate)
- Transfer fee you pay: 5,000 KRW
- Government adds: 2.5% = 1,000 BDT
- **Family receives: 41,000 BDT** (original 40,000 + 1,000 bonus)

**Important facts**:
- ✅ Applies to all legal remittances
- ✅ Works for both bank and mobile wallet (bKash, Nagad)
- ✅ Automatic - you don't need to apply
- ✅ Added by Bangladesh side, not Korean service
- ✅ Confirmed to continue through 2026

> 📌 **Key lesson**: The 2.5% incentive helps offset fees and exchange rate costs. Always use legal remittance services to get this benefit!
