## Understanding Fees and Exchange Rates for Cambodia

The total cost of sending money to Cambodia has two parts: **transfer fees** and **exchange rate markup**. Understanding both helps you save money.

This is the flat fee charged by the remittance service.

- Bank transfers: 20,000 - 40,000 KRW per transfer

### Exchange Rate Markup (KRW to USD)

This is the hidden cost. The service buys USD at one rate and sells to you at a slightly higher rate.

**How it works**:
- Market mid-rate: 1 USD = 1,350 KRW (example)
- Service's rate to you: 1 USD = 1,365 KRW
- Markup: About 1.1%

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- 1.1% markup = 5,500 KRW lost to exchange rate
- This can be MORE than the transfer fee

**How to find the best rate**:
- Compare services on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)
- Check the actual USD amount recipient receives
- Don't just look at the fee - calculate total cost
- Exchange rates change daily

### Total Cost Comparison Example

**Sending 500,000 KRW to Cambodia**:

| Service Type | Transfer Fee | Exchange Rate (USD) | USD Received | Total Cost |
|--------------|--------------|---------------------|--------------|------------|
| Service A | 3,000 KRW | 1 USD = 1,370 KRW | 362 USD | 5.2% loss |
| Service B | 7,000 KRW | 1 USD = 1,350 KRW | 365 USD | 3.5% loss |
| Bank | 30,000 KRW | 1 USD = 1,380 KRW | 340 USD | 9.8% loss |

**Service B is cheapest** even with higher fee, because the exchange rate is much better.

**Key lesson**: Always compare the **total USD amount received**, not just the transfer fee.

### Avoid Scams

**Common scams to watch for**:

**Fake customer service**:
- Never trust anyone asking for your password
- Official support never asks for passwords or PINs
- Verify phone numbers on official website

**Phishing messages**:
- Don't click links in SMS or emails
- Go directly to official app or website
- Check sender address carefully

**Too-good-to-be-true rates**:
- If rate is much better than all other services, be suspicious
- Stick to licensed, regulated services
- Use services listed on RemitBuddy

**Fake apps**:
- Only download from official app stores
- Check app developer name
- Read reviews before downloading
- Look for verified badge

### What to Do if Something Goes Wrong

**Transfer delayed**:
1. Check tracking number in app
2. Wait 24-48 hours (most delays resolve automatically)
3. Contact customer service with reference number
4. Keep all receipts and screenshots

**Wrong amount received**:
1. Check exchange rate and fees on receipt
2. Review payment confirmation
3. Contact customer service immediately
4. Provide transaction details

**Money not received**:
1. Verify recipient bank account is correct
2. Check with recipient bank
3. Contact remittance service support
4. If no resolution, file complaint with Korean Financial Supervisory Service
