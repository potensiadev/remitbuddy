## 💰 Understanding Fees and Exchange Rates for Malaysia

The **total cost** of sending money to Malaysia has two parts: **transfer fee** (visible) and **exchange rate markup** (often hidden). Understanding both helps you save money.

### Transfer Fees

This is the flat fee charged by the remittance service, shown clearly when you send.

**Typical Fees for Korea to Malaysia Transfers**:
- Digital apps (Hanpass, E9Pay, Moin): **3,000-8,000 KRW** per transfer
- Traditional bank transfers: **20,000-40,000 KRW** per transfer
- Some services: **Free first transfer** (promotional)
- Cash pickup services: **Higher fees** (10,000-15,000 KRW)

**Fee Factors**:
- Transfer amount (some services have tiered fees)
- Service type (app vs bank vs cash pickup)
- Promotional offers
- Membership or loyalty programs

**Tips to Reduce Fees**:
- ✅ Use digital apps instead of banks (save 15,000-30,000 KRW)
- ✅ Look for "first transfer free" promotions
- ✅ Send larger amounts less often (save on per-transfer fees)
- ✅ Check for referral bonuses
- ✅ Compare fees on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)

> **Learn more**: Read our detailed guide on [how to pay the lowest remittance fee](/blog/how-to-pay-lowest-remittance-fee) with money-saving strategies.

### Exchange Rate Markup (The Hidden Cost)

This is the **bigger cost** that many people miss. The service buys MYR at one rate and sells to you at a slightly higher rate.

**How Exchange Rate Markup Works**:

1. **Market mid-rate** (the "real" rate you see on Google):
   - 1,000 KRW = 3.50 MYR (example)
2. **Service's rate to you**:
   - 1,000 KRW = 3.45 MYR
3. **Markup**:
   - Difference = 0.05 MYR per 1,000 KRW
   - Markup percentage = 1.4%

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- Market rate would give: 1,750 MYR
- Service rate gives: 1,725 MYR
- **You lose: 25 MYR** (about 7,700 KRW) to exchange rate markup

> ⚠️ **Important**: The exchange rate markup can cost you MORE than the transfer fee!

**How to Find the Best Exchange Rate**:
1. Check the market mid-rate on Google or XE.com
2. Compare each service's rate on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)
3. Calculate the actual MYR amount family receives
4. Choose the service with the **most MYR received**, not lowest fee
5. Remember: exchange rates change daily, so compare before each transfer

### Total Cost Comparison Example

**Scenario: Sending 1,000,000 KRW to Malaysia**

**Analysis**:

- Even though Service A has low fee, the **exchange rate** makes the big difference

**Key Lesson**: Always compare the **total MYR amount received**, not just the transfer fee!

## 🔒 Safety and Security Tips

### Protect Your Remittance Account

**Use Strong Security**:
- ✅ Strong password (12+ characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
- ✅ Different password for each app (don't reuse)
- ✅ Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
- ✅ Use fingerprint or face recognition
- ✅ Keep your phone locked with PIN

**Never Share**:
- ❌ Your password or PIN
- ❌ SMS verification codes
- ❌ Login details
- ❌ Account information

### Verify Recipient Information

**Before First Transfer**:
1. Call your family to confirm bank details
2. Check name spelling matches bank account **exactly**
3. Verify account number digit by digit
4. Send small test amount first (50,000 KRW)
5. Confirm receipt before sending larger amounts
6. Save verified recipients for future use

**Double-Check Every Time**:
- Recipient name
- Bank name
- Account number
- Amount in MYR

### Avoid Common Scams

**❌ Fake Customer Service Scam**:
- Scammers call pretending to be from remittance company
- Ask for your password or verification code
- **Real companies NEVER ask for passwords**
- Always verify phone numbers on official website
- Hang up and call official number if suspicious

**❌ Phishing Messages**:
- Fake SMS or emails with links
- Claim there's a problem with your transfer
- Link takes you to fake website to steal login
- **Never click links** in SMS or email
- Go directly to official app or website

**❌ Too-Good-To-Be-True Rates**:
- Someone offers exchange rate much better than all services
- Likely a scam to steal your money
- Stick to **licensed, regulated services**
- Use services listed on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)

**❌ Fake Apps**:
- Scammers create fake apps that look real
- Steal your money and information
- **Only download from official app stores** (Google Play, Apple App Store)
- Check app developer name carefully
- Read reviews before downloading
- Look for verified developer badge

### What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

**Transfer Delayed or Not Received**:
1. Check tracking number in app for status
2. Wait 24-48 hours (most delays resolve automatically)
3. Ask family to check with their Malaysian bank
4. Contact customer service with reference number
5. Provide transaction details and screenshots
6. If no resolution after 3 days, file complaint with Korean Financial Supervisory Service

**Wrong Amount Received**:
1. Check exchange rate and fees on your receipt
2. Calculate expected amount
3. Review payment confirmation
4. Contact customer service immediately with details
5. Provide transaction reference number

**Money Sent to Wrong Account**:
1. Contact customer service **immediately**
2. Provide transaction details
3. May be able to recall if not yet processed
4. If processed, very difficult to recover
5. This is why you must **double-check before confirming**!
