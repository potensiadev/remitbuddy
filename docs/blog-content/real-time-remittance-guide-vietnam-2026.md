## How Real-time Remittance Works

The new instant remittance system uses advanced technology to eliminate delays. Here's the step-by-step process:

### Step 1: Digital Connection

Korea's payment system connects directly with partner countries. No intermediary banks are needed, which eliminates the biggest cause of delays.

### Step 2: Instant Verification

The system verifies your identity and transfer details instantly. This process takes under 30 seconds using secure digital verification.

### Step 3: Real-time Processing

Money moves through secure digital channels directly to the recipient's bank. The recipient bank receives funds immediately without manual processing.

### Step 4: Instant Notification

Both sender and receiver get instant notifications via SMS and app alerts. You know exactly when money arrives—no more anxious waiting.
