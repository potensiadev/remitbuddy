# Vietnam Tet 2027: When to Send Money for Best Rates

Tet (Vietnamese Lunar New Year) is the biggest holiday of the year. Your family needs money for:
- New clothes for the kids
- Food for the feast
- Red envelopes (lì xì) for relatives
- Travel back to hometown

But here's what many people don't know: **when you send money for Tet matters as much as how much you send.**

Send at the wrong time, and you'll pay the "Tet Premium" - worse exchange rates because everyone is sending money at once.

This guide shows you exactly when to send money for **Tet 2027** to get the best rates.

## 💰 The "Tet Premium": How Much It Costs You

### What Is the Tet Premium?

When everyone sends money at the same time:
- Demand for VND increases
- Exchange rate providers adjust rates
- You get **less VND for your KRW**

### Real Cost Example: ₩2,000,000 Transfer

| When You Send | Rate | Family Receives | Difference |
|---------------|------|-----------------|------------|
| **Early December** | 20.00 | 40,000,000 VND | Best |
| **Early January** | 19.90 | 39,800,000 VND | -200,000 VND |
| **Jan 15-20** | 19.70 | 39,400,000 VND | -600,000 VND |
| **Jan 21-28** | 19.50 | 39,000,000 VND | -1,000,000 VND |

**Waiting until the last week costs you 1,000,000 VND** (about ₩50,000)

### Historical Pattern

| 6 weeks before Tet | Normal rates |
| 4 weeks before Tet | 0.5% worse |
| 2 weeks before Tet | 1-2% worse |
| 1 week before Tet | 2-3% worse |
| During Tet | Transfers delayed |

## 🎯 Tet Money Sending Strategy

### Strategy 1: Send Everything Early (Best)

**When**: November - December 15
**What**: Send all your Tet money at once

**Advantages**:
- Best exchange rates
- No last-minute stress
- Family can budget
- Single fee payment

**Example**:
- Tet budget: ₩3,000,000
- Send in November: Get ~59,700,000 VND
- Send in January: Get ~58,200,000 VND
- **You save: 1,500,000 VND**

### Strategy 2: Split Early and Late (Moderate)

**When**: 70% in December, 30% in January
**What**: Send most early, keep some for last-minute needs

**Advantages**:
- Most money at good rates
- Flexibility for unexpected needs
- Covers both planned and surprise expenses

**Example**:
- Total: ₩3,000,000
- December: ₩2,100,000 → ~41,790,000 VND (good rate)
- January 10: ₩900,000 → ~17,820,000 VND (OK rate)
- **Total received: ~59,610,000 VND**

### Strategy 3: Last Minute (Avoid!)

**When**: January 20+
**What**: Send everything right before Tet

**Problems**:
- Worst exchange rates
- Possible delays
- Family stress
- Higher hidden fees

**Example**:
- Total: ₩3,000,000
- January 25: Get only ~58,000,000 VND
- **You lose: 1,700,000 VND** compared to early sending

## ⚠️ Tet Transfer Warnings

### Warning 1: Don't Wait for "Better Rates"

Some people think: "Maybe rates will improve if I wait."

**Reality**: Rates almost always get WORSE as Tet approaches. Don't gamble.

### Warning 2: Vietnamese Bank Closures

**January 29 - February 2**: Most Vietnamese banks are closed.

If you send during this time:
- Money may sit in limbo
- Family can't access until Feb 3+
- MoMo works, but withdrawal limits apply

### Warning 3: App Delays During Peak

Even remittance apps can slow down during Tet rush:
- Normal: 10-30 minutes
- Tet week: 1-4 hours possible
- Tet holiday: Next business day

### Warning 4: Don't Use Bank Wire

Korean banks + Tet + SWIFT = nightmare scenario

- 2-3 days normally → 5-7 days during Tet
- Intermediary banks may be closed
- Your family won't have money for Tet

## ❓ FAQ: Tet Money Transfers

### When is the best time to send money for Tet 2027?

**November to mid-December** is best. You get normal exchange rates and avoid the Tet premium that starts in January.

### How much worse are rates during Tet?

Typically **1-3% worse** in the two weeks before Tet. For a ₩2,000,000 transfer, that's 400,000-1,200,000 VND lost.

### Can I send money during Tet holiday?

You can send via apps, but Vietnamese banks are closed January 29 - February 2. Your family may not receive it until February 3+.

### What if I forgot and it's already January 25?

Send immediately via Hanpass or E9Pay to MoMo. Accept the worse rate. Something is better than nothing for your family.

### How much should I send for Tet?

Typical range: **₩500,000 - ₩2,000,000** depending on your family's needs. Consider food, clothes, red envelopes, and travel.

### Is MoMo or bank better for Tet transfers?

**MoMo is better** for Tet because:
- Faster (5-10 minutes)
- Works during bank closures
- Family can pay directly with MoMo
- Easy to share with relatives

### Should I send in KRW or USD?

Send in **KRW**. Converting KRW → USD → VND costs you twice on exchange rates.
