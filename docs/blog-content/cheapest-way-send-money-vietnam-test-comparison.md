## 🤔 Surprises and Lessons Learned

### Surprise 1: MoMo is Significantly Faster

I expected maybe 5 minutes difference. The actual gap was **16 minutes** (8 min vs 24 min). For emergencies, MoMo is clearly better.

### Surprise 2: Bank Hidden Fees Were Worse Than Expected

The ₩25,000 "transfer fee" was just the start. SWIFT fee (₩8,000) and intermediary deduction ($5) added up. **True bank cost was 60% higher than displayed.**

### Surprise 3: Rate Differences Are Small Between Apps

Hanpass, E9Pay, and GME rates were within 0.3% of each other. The fee difference mattered more for this amount.

### Surprise 4: GME Is Slower But Might Be Better for Large Amounts

At ₩1,000,000, GME wasn't the best choice. But I've heard they offer better rates for ₩3,000,000+. I'll test this next time.

### Surprise 5: The Bank Experience Was Painful

- Wait at branch: 20 minutes
- Fill forms: 10 minutes
- Processing: 52 hours
- Result: Least money received

I genuinely don't understand why anyone uses bank wires for Vietnam anymore.

## ❓ FAQ From This Experiment

### Why didn't you test Wise?

Wise isn't available for Korea → Vietnam transfers in the same way. It's not a fair comparison.

### Couldn't the rates have changed between transfers?

Yes, that's a limitation. I sent all transfers within 1 hour to minimize this, but there could be small variations. The relative differences are still meaningful.

### Is Hanpass always the best?

For ₩1,000,000 transfers, it was best on this day. Rates change daily. Always compare on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) before sending.

### Why was the bank so much worse?

Multiple factors:
- Higher base fee
- Additional SWIFT fee
- Intermediary bank deduction
- Worse exchange rate (banks use wider spreads)

### Would results be different for ₩500,000?

Likely similar rankings. The percentage costs would be higher since fees are mostly flat.

### Can I trust these results?

These are my real transfers with real money. I have receipts for all of them. But your experience may vary slightly based on timing, amount, and luck.
