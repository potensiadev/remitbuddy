The good news? You have many options today. Digital remittance services offer better rates and faster transfers than traditional banks. Let's explore your options.

## Common Fees When Sending Money to Nepal

### Transfer Fee

This is what the service charges to send money. Usually:
- 3,000 - 10,000 KRW for digital apps
- 20,000 - 30,000 KRW for banks
- 5,000 - 15,000 KRW for Western Union (depends on amount)

### Exchange Rate Markup

This is the hidden cost. Services buy currency at one rate and sell to you at a different rate. The difference is their profit.

**Example**:
- Real market rate (mid-market): 1 KRW = 0.078 NPR
- Service's rate to you: 1 KRW = 0.076 NPR
- You lose: 2.6% on the exchange rate

Good services have small markups (0.5-2%). Bad services have large markups (3-7%).

### Korean Remittance Tax

Good news! Digital transfers usually don't have the 1% Korean remittance tax. Always confirm this with your service.

### What is the cheapest way to send money from Korea to Nepal?

Yes! Many services including eSewa Money Transfer, Remitly, and others allow you to send money directly to an eSewa wallet. The money arrives instantly and your recipient can use it immediately for payments or withdraw cash.
