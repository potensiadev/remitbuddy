## Understanding Fees and Exchange Rates

The total cost of sending money to GCash has two parts: **transfer fees** and **exchange rate markup**. Understanding both helps you save money.

### Transfer Fees

This is the flat fee charged by the remittance service.

**Typical fees for Korea to Philippines GCash transfers**:
- Digital apps: 3,000 - 8,000 KRW per transfer
- Bank transfers: 20,000 - 35,000 KRW per transfer
- Some services: Free for first transfer

**Tips to reduce fees**:
- Look for promotional offers
- Send larger amounts less frequently (but only if you can afford it)
- Use services with lower fixed fees for small amounts
- Check if loyalty programs exist

### Exchange Rate Markup

This is the hidden cost. The service buys PHP at one rate and sells to you at a slightly higher rate. The difference is their profit.

**How it works**:
- Market mid-rate: 1 KRW = 0.048 PHP (example)
- Service's rate to you: 1 KRW = 0.047 PHP
- Markup: About 2%

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- 2% markup = 10,000 KRW lost to exchange rate
- This is often MORE than the transfer fee

**How to find the best rate**:
- Compare services on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com)
- Check the actual PHP amount your recipient receives
- Don't just look at the fee - calculate total cost
- Exchange rates change daily, so check each time

> **Read more**: Learn [how to find the best exchange rate in Korea](/blog/how-to-find-best-exchange-rate-in-korea) with detailed comparisons and tips.

### Total Cost Comparison Example

**Sending 500,000 KRW to GCash**:

| Service Type | Transfer Fee | Exchange Rate | PHP Received | Total Cost |
|--------------|--------------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Service A | 3,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.047 PHP | 23,359 PHP | 4.2% loss |
| Service B | 5,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.048 PHP | 23,760 PHP | 2.1% loss |

**Service B is cheapest** even though it has a higher fee, because the exchange rate is better.

**Key lesson**: Always compare the **total PHP amount received**, not just the transfer fee.
