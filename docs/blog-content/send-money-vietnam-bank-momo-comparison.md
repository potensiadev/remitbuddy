# Send Money to Vietnam: Bank vs MoMo vs Cash Pickup

Over 200,000 Vietnamese workers in Korea send money home regularly. But which method is best—bank transfer, MoMo wallet, or cash pickup?

The answer depends on your amount, speed needs, and how your family uses the money.

---

## Quick Answer: Which Should You Choose?

| Your Situation | Best Option | Why |
|----------------|-------------|-----|
| Regular monthly support (1-3M KRW) | **Bank Transfer** | Lowest fees, safest, best rates |
| Family uses mobile payments | **Bank → MoMo** | Automatic transfer to wallet |
| Urgent emergency | **Cash Pickup** | Available within hours |
| Large amount (5M+ KRW) | **Bank Transfer** | Best exchange rates for big amounts |
| Rural area, no bank nearby | **Cash Pickup** | No bank account needed |

## Option 2: MoMo Wallet (via Bank Transfer)

### How It Works
1. You send money to a Vietnamese bank account
2. If bank is linked to MoMo, money transfers automatically
3. Family uses MoMo for payments, bills, shopping

### Best For
- Families who use mobile payments
- Regular small transfers
- Recipients who prefer digital money

### Key Point
You cannot send directly to MoMo. You send to a bank account that is linked to MoMo.

### Setup (One-time, 2 minutes)
1. Your family opens MoMo app
2. Goes to "My Wallet" → "Linked Bank Account"
3. Selects their bank and enters account number
4. Verifies with OTP

After this, every bank transfer appears in MoMo automatically.

### Costs
Same as bank transfer—no extra fees for MoMo linking.

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Instant from bank to MoMo
- Over 100,000+ payment points in Vietnam
- Pay bills, shop, send money within Vietnam
- 50+ million MoMo users

**Cons:**
- Still requires bank account
- Same 4-24 hour transfer time to bank
- Bank-MoMo linking required

## Option 3: Cash Pickup

### How It Works
1. You send money and get a reference number
2. Recipient goes to pickup location with ID
3. Receives cash in VND immediately

### Best For
- Emergencies (available within hours)
- Recipients without bank accounts
- Rural areas with limited banking

### Pickup Networks in Vietnam
- Western Union (major cities)
- MoneyGram (limited locations)
- Local bank branches (varies by service)

### Costs (Sending 1,000,000 KRW)

| Service | Fee | Speed | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|-------|
| GME Remit | 8,000-15,000 KRW | 1-4 hours | Limited Vietnam locations |
| Bank wire + cash | 25,000+ KRW | 1-2 days | Recipient withdraws at bank |

*Cash pickup typically costs 2-3x more than bank transfer.*

### Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Fastest option (hours, not days)
- No bank account required
- Cash in hand immediately

**Cons:**
- Higher fees
- Lower exchange rates
- Recipient must travel to location
- Security risk carrying cash
- Limited to business hours

## FAQ

**Can I send directly to MoMo from Korea?**

No. You send to a Vietnamese bank account. If linked to MoMo, money transfers automatically to the wallet.

**Which is faster—bank or MoMo?**

Same speed to the bank (4-24 hours). Once in the bank, MoMo transfer is instant if linked.

**Is cash pickup safe?**

The transfer is safe. But carrying large amounts of cash has risks. For regular transfers, bank is safer.

**What if my family doesn't have a bank account?**

Cash pickup is the only option. Or help them open a bank account—most services require only Vietnamese ID.

**Which has better exchange rates?**

Bank transfer always has better rates than cash pickup. MoMo (via bank) has the same rate as bank transfer.

**Can I use multiple methods?**

Yes. Many workers use bank transfer for regular support and cash pickup for emergencies only.
