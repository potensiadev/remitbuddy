## ⚠️ Night Transfer Warnings

### Warning 1: Customer Support May Be Unavailable

| Time (Korea) | Support Availability |
|--------------|---------------------|
| 9 AM - 6 PM | ✅ Full support |
| 6 PM - 9 PM | 🔶 Limited |
| 9 PM - 9 AM | ⚠️ Minimal or none |

**Solution**: Double-check all details before sending. Fixing mistakes at night is harder.

### Warning 2: Bank Accounts May Show Delay

Even if the app says "Completed," Vietnamese banks may not credit until morning.

| App Status | Reality |
|------------|---------|
| "Completed" at 2 AM | Money is sent |
| Bank shows nothing | Normal - bank updates in morning |
| Family gets it | When VN bank opens (~8-9 AM Vietnam) |

### Warning 3: Exchange Rates May Differ

Some services use "overnight rates" that may be slightly different from daytime rates.

| Time | Rate Quality |
|------|--------------|
| 9 AM - 5 PM | Best rates (peak liquidity) |
| 5 PM - 9 PM | Good rates |
| 9 PM - 6 AM | Rates may be slightly worse |

**Impact**: Usually less than 0.5% difference. For emergencies, don't worry about it.

### Warning 4: Verification Issues at Night

If the app requires additional verification (large amount, new recipient), it may be delayed until morning when staff are available.

**Solution**: For large or first-time transfers, send during daytime if possible.

## ❓ FAQ: Night Transfers to Vietnam

### Can I send money to Vietnam at 2 AM?

Yes. Apps like Hanpass and E9Pay work 24/7. For fastest delivery at night, send to MoMo wallet.

### Will my family receive money at night?

- **MoMo**: Yes, instantly (5-10 minutes)
- **Bank account**: Depends. The transfer processes, but Vietnamese bank credits when they open (8-9 AM Vietnam time).

### Are night transfer fees higher?

Usually no. Fees are the same 24/7. Exchange rates might be very slightly worse at night, but the difference is minimal.

### What if something goes wrong at night?

Customer support is limited at night. If there's an issue:
1. Check app status - most issues resolve automatically
2. Wait until morning for support if needed
3. For emergencies, try the app's emergency contact if available

### Which app is best for midnight transfers?

**Hanpass to MoMo** or **E9Pay to MoMo**. Both work 24/7 and deliver in 5-10 minutes, even at midnight.

### Do weekend nights work the same?

Yes for apps. MoMo works 24/7, 365 days. Bank accounts may not credit until Monday morning (Vietnam time) if you send Saturday night.

### Is it safe to send money at night?

Yes. The apps use the same security 24/7. Just double-check recipient details since support is limited for fixing mistakes at night.
