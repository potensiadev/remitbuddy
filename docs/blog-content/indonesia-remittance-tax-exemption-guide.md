## What is the Indonesia Remittance Tax Exemption?

The **Indonesia remittance tax exemption** is a new policy that started in January 2026. Here's how it works:

- **No tax on remittances under $1,000 per transfer**
- **Recipients don't pay income tax on these amounts**
- **Multiple transfers allowed each month**
- **Applies to all official money transfer services**

Before this policy, Indonesia taxed remittances as income. Recipients paid 5-15% tax on received money. Now, transfers under $1,000 are completely tax-free.

### Key Benefits of the New Policy

- Faster processing without tax verification
- More money stays in your family's pocket

Yes, the tax exemption applies to all official, licensed money transfer services including banks, online services, and money transfer operators.

Yes, you can send multiple transfers each month. Each transfer under $1,000 is tax-free. There's no monthly limit on the number of transfers.

The exemption is for amounts **under** $1,000. To be safe, send $999 or less to ensure tax-free status.

No, the exemption is automatic. Recipients don't need to file paperwork or apply for the exemption.
