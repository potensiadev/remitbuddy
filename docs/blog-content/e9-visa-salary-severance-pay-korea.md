## ⏱️ How to Calculate Overtime Pay (1.5x Rate)

Overtime pay is one of the most important parts of E-9 worker income. Many workers do overtime but don't know if they're paid correctly.

### Korean Labor Law on Overtime

**Legal requirements:**
- Regular work week: **40 hours maximum**
- Regular work day: **8 hours maximum**
- Overtime is **voluntary** (you can refuse)
- Overtime pay: **1.5x your normal hourly rate**
- Weekend work: **1.5x rate**
- Holiday work: **2.0x rate** (or 1.5x if employer gives substitute holiday)

### Step-by-Step Overtime Calculation

Let's calculate overtime pay correctly:

#### Step 1: Calculate Your Hourly Rate

**Formula**: Monthly Salary ÷ 209 hours = Hourly Rate

**Example**: Base salary ₩2,200,000/month
- ₩2,200,000 ÷ 209 hours = **₩10,526 per hour**

#### Step 2: Calculate Overtime Rate

**Formula**: Hourly Rate × 1.5 = Overtime Rate

**Example**: Regular rate ₩10,526/hour
- ₩10,526 × 1.5 = **₩15,789 per overtime hour**

#### Step 3: Calculate Monthly Overtime Pay

**Formula**: Overtime Hours × Overtime Rate = Overtime Pay

**Example**: 50 hours overtime per month
- 50 hours × ₩15,789 = **₩789,450 overtime pay**

## 💼 Severance Pay: Your Rights When Contract Ends

**Severance pay** (퇴직금, toejikgeum) is money your employer MUST pay you when you finish working. This is required by Korean law.

### Who Qualifies for Severance Pay?

You get severance pay if:
- ✅ Worked at the same company for **1 year or more**
- ✅ Worked average of **15 hours or more per week**
- ✅ Contract ends (finished, not renewed, or terminated)

**All E-9 workers who complete their contract qualify.**

### How Much Severance Pay Do You Get?

**Formula**: (Average Monthly Wage × Years Worked)

**Simple Calculation**:
- 1 year of work = 1 month of average wage
- 2 years = 2 months of average wage
- 3 years = 3 months of average wage

### Step-by-Step Severance Pay Calculation

#### Step 1: Calculate Average Monthly Wage

**Formula**: Total wages in last 3 months ÷ 3

**Include:**
- ✅ Base salary
- ✅ Overtime pay
- ✅ Holiday pay
- ✅ Night shift bonuses
- ✅ Regular allowances

**Exclude:**
- ❌ One-time bonuses
- ❌ Reimbursements
- ❌ Irregular payments

#### Step 2: Calculate Total Work Period

Count from first day to last day of work. Round to the nearest month.

- Less than 15 days = No change
- 15 days or more = Count as full month

**Example**: Worked 3 years 7 months = 3.58 years

#### Step 3: Apply Formula

**Severance Pay** = Average Monthly Wage × Years Worked

### When Do You Receive Severance Pay?

**Timeline:**
- **Legal requirement**: Within 14 days of your last working day
- **Typical**: 7-14 days after contract end
- **Payment method**: Bank transfer to your Korean account

**What if employer doesn't pay?**
1. Request in writing (have interpreter help)
2. Contact labor support center (외국인근로자지원센터)
3. File complaint with Ministry of Employment and Labor
4. Severance pay is your legal right - you will get it

> ⚠️ **Warning**: Some employers try to avoid paying severance by claiming you "quit voluntarily." Make sure your contract completion is documented properly.

## ⚠️ Common Salary Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Not Checking Overtime Pay

Many E-9 workers don't verify overtime calculations. You might be underpaid.

**Solution**: Calculate your own overtime pay using formulas above. Compare with payslip.

### Mistake 2: Not Understanding Deductions

Some employers deduct too much or deduct items that are illegal.

**Legal deductions only:**
- ✅ National Pension (4.5%)
- ✅ Health Insurance (3.545%)
- ✅ Employment Insurance (0.9%)
- ✅ Income Tax (varies by income)
- ✅ Dormitory (if agreed in contract)

**Illegal deductions:**
- ❌ "Training fees" after you start work
- ❌ Excessive charges for tools or uniforms
- ❌ Penalties for quitting (if you give proper notice)

### Mistake 3: Not Claiming Severance Pay

Some employers don't automatically pay severance. You must request it.

**Solution**: At end of contract, request severance pay in writing. Keep copy of request.

### Mistake 4: Using Banks for Remittance

Banks take ₩20,000-35,000 fee plus bad exchange rates. You lose big money.

**Solution**: Use [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) to compare remittance services and save.

### Mistake 5: Not Filing Tax Refund

E-9 workers can get ₩500,000 - ₩1.5 million tax refund yearly. Many don't file.

**Solution**: File year-end tax settlement every January-March.

### Mistake 6: Working Too Much Overtime

Some workers work 70-80 hours overtime monthly. This damages health.

**Solution**: Balance income and health. 40-50 hours overtime is reasonable.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**💬 Q: What is the average E-9 visa salary in Korea?**

A: Average E-9 salary is ₩2.4-3.0 million per month including overtime. Base salary is typically ₩2.0-2.3 million (minimum wage or slightly above), with overtime adding ₩400,000-800,000. Construction and manufacturing workers generally earn more than agriculture or service industry workers.

**💬 Q: How is overtime pay calculated for E-9 workers?**

A: Overtime pay is 1.5x your regular hourly rate. Calculate: (Monthly Salary ÷ 209 hours) × 1.5 × Overtime Hours. For example, if your base salary is ₩2,200,000, your hourly rate is ₩10,526, and overtime rate is ₩15,789 per hour.

**💬 Q: How much severance pay do E-9 workers get?**

A: You get 1 month of average wage for each year worked. After 3 years with ₩2.5 million average monthly wage, you'd receive ₩7.5 million severance pay. After 4 years and 8 months at ₩3 million average wage, you'd get about ₩14 million.

**💬 Q: When do I receive severance pay?**

A: Employers must pay within 14 days of your last working day. It's typically paid by bank transfer 7-14 days after contract completion. If your employer doesn't pay, contact the Ministry of Employment and Labor or your local labor support center.

**💬 Q: Can E-9 workers refuse overtime?**

A: Yes, overtime is voluntary in Korea. You can refuse without penalty. However, many E-9 workers accept overtime to increase income and send more money home. Typical overtime is 40-60 hours per month.

**💬 Q: How much can E-9 workers send home monthly?**

A: After deductions and living expenses, most E-9 workers can send ₩1.5-2.6 million home per month ($1,120-1,950 USD). This depends on overtime hours, living arrangements, and personal expenses. Average workers send ₩2.0-2.1 million monthly.

**💬 Q: Do E-9 workers pay income tax in Korea?**

A: Yes, income tax is automatically deducted from your salary. The amount varies by income level, typically ₩50,000-180,000 per month. You can get a tax refund by filing year-end tax settlement (January-March). Most E-9 workers get ₩500,000-1,500,000 back.

**💬 Q: What deductions come from E-9 salary?**

A: Legal deductions are: National Pension (4.5%), Health Insurance (3.545%), Employment Insurance (0.9%), and Income Tax (varies). For ₩2.5 million salary, total deductions are about ₩280,000-350,000. Dormitory fees (if applicable) are additional ₩150,000-300,000.

**💬 Q: Can I check if my employer is paying me correctly?**

A: Yes, use the formulas in this guide to calculate your own pay. Your monthly payslip should show: base salary, overtime hours and pay, deductions, and net income. If numbers don't match, contact your labor support center (외국인근로자지원센터) for free help.

**💬 Q: What happens to severance pay if I change employers?**

A: If you work 1+ year at each employer, you get severance from each company. For example, 2 years at Company A (₩5 million severance) + 1.5 years at Company B (₩3.75 million severance) = ₩8.75 million total.

**💬 Q: How much can I save over 3 years on E-9 visa?**

A: Including regular monthly remittances and severance pay, average E-9 workers send home ₩64-104 million over 3 years ($48,000-78,000 USD). This includes: ₩2 million monthly × 36 months (₩72 million) + severance pay (₩7-10 million) minus living expenses.
