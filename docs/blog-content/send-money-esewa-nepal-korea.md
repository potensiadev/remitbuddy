## ❓ Can You Send Money Directly to eSewa from Korea?

**Short answer**: Not directly, but easily through a 2-step process.

Most Korean remittance services don't send directly to eSewa wallets. Instead, they send to:
1. **Bank accounts** in Nepal
2. **Cash pickup** locations
3. **IME Pay** or other mobile wallets (some services)

**Here's how it works**:

### Method 1: Send to Bank Account, Then to eSewa (Most Common)

1. 📤 You send money from Korea to recipient's Nepali bank account
2. 🏦 Money arrives in bank account (same day to 1 day)
3. 💸 Your family transfers from bank to eSewa (instant, free)

**Time**: 10 minutes to 1 business day total
**Cost**: Only the remittance fee + exchange rate

> 💡 **Tip**: Make sure your family has their bank account linked to their eSewa. They can do this in the eSewa app in 5 minutes.

### Method 2: Cash Pickup to eSewa

1. 📤 You send money for cash pickup in Nepal
2. 👤 Recipient picks up cash at IME, Prabhu, or other agents
3. 💸 Deposit cash to eSewa at any agent

**Time**: Same day to next day
**Cost**: Remittance fee + possible agent fee

### Method 3: Some Services Support Direct Wallet Transfer

A few remittance services are starting to offer direct transfers to Nepali mobile wallets including eSewa or IME Pay. Check with your service if this is available.

**The good news**: Method 1 is easy, fast, and most families already have their bank linked to eSewa, so the transfer from bank to eSewa is instant and free.

## 💰 Understanding Fees and Exchange Rates

The total cost of sending money to Nepal (and eSewa) has two main parts: transfer fees and exchange rate markup. Let's break it down.

### Transfer Fees

This is the flat fee charged by the remittance service.

**Typical fees for Korea to Nepal transfers**:
- 💵 Digital apps: 3,000 - 8,000 KRW per transfer
- 🏦 Bank transfers: 20,000 - 35,000 KRW per transfer
- 🎁 Some services: Free or discounted for first transfer

**Fee comparison by amount**:

| Amount Sending | Typical Fee | Fee as % |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| 100,000 KRW | 3,000-5,000 KRW | 3-5% |
| 500,000 KRW | 4,000-6,000 KRW | 0.8-1.2% |
| 1,000,000 KRW | 5,000-8,000 KRW | 0.5-0.8% |

**Tips to reduce fees**:
- ✅ Look for promotional offers (first transfer free)
- ✅ Send larger amounts less frequently (if you can afford it)
- ✅ Use services with lower fixed fees for small amounts
- ✅ Check for loyalty programs or referral bonuses

### Exchange Rate Markup

This is the hidden cost. Services buy NPR at one rate and sell to you at a slightly different rate. The difference is their profit.

**How it works**:
- 📊 Market mid-rate: 1 KRW = 0.078 NPR (example)
- 💱 Service's rate to you: 1 KRW = 0.076 NPR
- 📉 Markup: About 2.6% hidden cost

**On a 500,000 KRW transfer**:
- 2% markup = 10,000 KRW lost to exchange rate
- This is often MORE than the transfer fee

**How to find the best rate**:
- ✅ Compare services on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) before each transfer
- ✅ Check the actual NPR amount your recipient receives
- ✅ Don't just look at the fee - calculate total cost
- ✅ Rates change daily, so check each time

> 💡 **Learn more**: Understand [why remittance amounts vary](/blog/why-remittance-amounts-vary) and [how to find the best exchange rate](/blog/how-to-find-best-exchange-rate-in-korea) with detailed explanations.

### Total Cost Comparison Example

**Sending 500,000 KRW to Nepal Bank (then to eSewa)**:

| Service | Transfer Fee | Exchange Rate | NPR Received | Total Cost |
|---------|--------------|---------------|--------------|------------|
| Service A | 3,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.076 NPR | 37,772 NPR | 4.6% loss |
| Service B | 5,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.078 NPR | 38,610 NPR | 2.8% loss |
| Bank | 25,000 KRW | 1 KRW = 0.074 NPR | 35,150 NPR | 11.3% loss |

**Service B is cheapest** even though it has a higher fee, because the exchange rate is much better.

**Key lesson**: Always compare the **total NPR amount received**, not just the transfer fee.

Protecting your money and information is important. Follow these safety guidelines.

### Choose Licensed Services Only

- Hanpass, GME Remit, E9Pay, Gmoneytrans, Moin, Coinshot, JRF are all licensed

❌ **Never use**:

- Friends of friends offering "better rates"

### Protect Your Account Information

**Create strong security**:
- 🔐 Use strong, unique passwords (12+ characters)
- 📱 Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
- 🔒 Use fingerprint or face recognition
- 📵 Never share passwords or PINs with anyone

**Avoid sharing**:
- ❌ Password
- ❌ SMS verification codes
- ❌ Login details
- ❌ Account PINs
- ❌ Transaction screenshots with sensitive info

### Verify Recipient Information Carefully

Before sending, always:
- ✅ Call your family to confirm bank account details
- ✅ Check spelling of recipient name (must match bank account exactly)
- ✅ Verify bank name and account number
- ✅ Test with small amount first if new recipient
- ✅ Save verified recipients for future use

> ⚠️ **Critical**: Wrong spelling or wrong account number can cause delays, rejections, or even lost money. Always double-check!

### Avoid Common Scams

**Fake customer service scam**:
- ❌ Never trust anyone asking for your password
- ❌ Official support NEVER asks for passwords or PINs
- ✅ Always verify phone numbers on official website

**Phishing message scam**:
- ❌ Don't click links in SMS or emails claiming problems with transfer
- ✅ Go directly to official app or website
- ✅ Check sender address carefully

**"Better rate" scam**:
- ❌ If someone offers much better rates than all services, it's a scam
- ✅ Stick to licensed, regulated services
- ✅ Use services listed on RemitBuddy

**Fake app scam**:
- ❌ Fake apps exist that look like real remittance services
- ✅ Only download from official Google Play or App Store
- ✅ Check developer name matches official company
- ✅ Read reviews before downloading
