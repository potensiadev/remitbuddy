## 📊 Exchange Rate Comparison: Where You Really Save

Here's a secret: **exchange rates matter more than fees** for larger amounts. Even a 0.5% better exchange rate can save you more than a lower fee.

### Understanding Exchange Rate Markup

All remittance services make money in two ways:
1. **Transfer fee** (what they show you)
2. **Exchange rate markup** (the hidden profit)

**How markup works:**
- Real market rate (mid-market): 1 USD = 1,350 KRW
- Service buys USD at: 1,350 KRW
- Service sells to you at: 1,365 KRW
- **Markup**: 15 KRW per dollar (about 1.1%)

### Typical Exchange Rate Markups

| Service | Typical Markup | Rate Quality |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| **Hanpass** | 0.5% - 1.5% | Very competitive |
| **GME Remit** | 1.0% - 2.0% | Competitive |
| **E9Pay** | 1.0% - 2.0% | Competitive |
| **Korean Banks** | 2.0% - 4.0% | Poor |
| **Airport Exchange** | 5.0% - 10.0% | Very poor |

### Real Exchange Rate Impact

Let's see how exchange rate markup affects a 500,000 KRW transfer to USD:

**Example: Sending to Philippines (KRW to PHP)**

Market mid-rate: 1 KRW = 0.048 PHP

| Service | Their Rate | Markup | PHP Received | Difference |
|---------|-----------|--------|--------------|------------|
| **Hanpass** | 0.0472 PHP | 1.7% | 23,100 PHP | Best |
| **GME Remit** | 0.0470 PHP | 2.1% | 23,000 PHP | -100 PHP |
| **E9Pay** | 0.0469 PHP | 2.3% | 22,950 PHP | -150 PHP |
| **Korean Bank** | 0.0460 PHP | 4.2% | 22,540 PHP | -560 PHP |

**Result**: Hanpass's better rate gives you 150-560 PHP more even if their fee is the same!

**On 12 months:**
- Hanpass vs Bank: 6,720 PHP saved (about 175,000 KRW)
- This is MORE than the fee savings!

### Which Has the Best Exchange Rates?

Based on user reports and market data:

🥇 **Best: Hanpass** - Consistently offers rates closest to mid-market rate (0.5-1.5% markup)

🥈 **Good: GME Remit & E9Pay** - Competitive rates (1.0-2.0% markup)

🥉 **Avoid: Banks** - Poor rates (2.0-4.0% markup)
