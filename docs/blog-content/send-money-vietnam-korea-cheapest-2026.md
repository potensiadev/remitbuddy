## ❓ FAQ: Sending Money to Vietnam from Korea

### How much does it cost to send money to Vietnam from Korea?

Using remittance apps like Hanpass or E9Pay, fees range from **₩3,000 to ₩5,000** (about $2-4 USD). Korean banks charge **₩20,000-₩30,000** ($15-25 USD).

### What is the cheapest way to send money to Vietnam?

For amounts under ₩2,000,000, **Hanpass** and **E9Pay** are usually cheapest. For larger amounts, compare rates on [RemitBuddy](https://www.remitbuddy.com) as GME Remit may offer better exchange rates.

### How long does it take to send money to Vietnam from Korea?

- **MoMo wallet**: 5-10 minutes
- **Bank transfer (Hanpass/E9Pay)**: 10-30 minutes
- **Bank transfer (Korean bank)**: 2-3 business days

### Can I send money to MoMo from Korea?

Yes! **Hanpass**, **E9Pay**, and **GME Remit** all support MoMo wallet transfers. Your family receives money in under 10 minutes.

### What is the maximum I can send to Vietnam?

Most services allow up to **$50,000 USD per year** for individual transfers. For larger amounts, contact the service directly.

### Do I need my recipient's bank account number?

For bank transfers, yes. For MoMo wallet transfers, you only need their **phone number** linked to MoMo.

### Is it better to send KRW or USD to Vietnam?

Send in **KRW**. Converting KRW → USD → VND loses money on two exchange rates. Direct KRW → VND conversion is cheaper.

### What documents do I need to send money to Vietnam?

- **ARC card** (Alien Registration Card)
- **Phone number** registered in Korea
- **Bank account** in your name (for some services)

### Can I send money to Vietnam on weekends?

Yes, but your family may receive it **Monday morning** if sent after banking hours. MoMo transfers work 24/7.
